SD card bus

Selects how the SD card is connected. SPI needs CLK, MOSI (CMD), MISO (D0) and CS (D3); 4-bit SDMMC additionally needs D1 and D2 and pull-ups on CMD and D0–D3. The change is stored on the device and applies after a restart.

Active mode: -

The device does not restart while an operation or recording is active. If the card does not mount in the selected mode, switch back here; the web interface works without an SD card.
